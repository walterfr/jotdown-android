Jotdown is an open-source document manager and PDF reader built for researchers, students and academics. Reading, annotating and organizing your documents works fully offline; network is only used for optional metadata lookup by DOI (CrossRef) or ISBN (Open Library) and dictionary downloads. Focused on privacy — no account required, no data leaves the device beyond what those optional features need.

Features:
* PDF text extraction, hierarchical outline/TOC navigation, and full-text PDF search via open-source PdfBox
* Sticky note annotations and vector drawings (stylus + palm rejection)
* Annotated PDF export merging vector drawings and sticky notes onto pages
* Academic citation (fichamento) generator with ABNT NBR 6023 and APA 7th edition formatting
* Export notes and references to Markdown (.md), TXT, PDF, and BibTeX (.bib)
* Optional metadata lookup via DOI (CrossRef) and ISBN (Open Library)
* Local folder organization, reading status tracking, and research goals
* Offline dictionaries (Portuguese / English)
* Local backup and restore (ZIP)
* Dark mode support
